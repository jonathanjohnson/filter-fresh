## Publishing priority

Three tiers based on volume × intent × site authority lift. Publish one post per week starting at the top of Tier A.

### Tier A — high-volume, high-conversion (publish first)

1. **Pool filter pressure gauge: what the numbers actually mean** (vol 1,100, KD 0)
   Largest single-post opportunity. Pressure gauge confusion is the gateway to "do I need a cleaning?" Capture the AI Overview footprint.

2. **How often should you clean your pool filter in Southern California** (vol 200 + 40 from variants, KD 0)
   Top-funnel cornerstone. Localizes the answer (heat, dust, pollen) to make the page sticky.

3. **How much should pool filter cleaning cost in San Diego County** (vol 60, KD 0, $2.50 CPC)
   Highest commercial intent in the cluster. Direct funnel to `/pricing` and `/book`.

4. **Signs your pool filter needs cleaning right now** (intent overlap with "pool filter pressure too high")
   Symptom-driven. Catches urgent searches like "pool filter pressure too high" and "pool cloudy water filter."

### Tier B — supporting education (publish second)

5. **How a pool filter actually works (cartridge, DE, sand explained)** (vol 20, KD 1, TP 250)
   Cornerstone of Pillar 1. Internal-link target for every other post in the cluster.

6. **Filter cleaning vs filter replacement: when to do which**
   Disambiguates a common DIY decision. Pairs well with the cartridge replacement post.

7. **Cartridge replacement schedule: when cleaning is not enough** (vol 20, "when to replace pool filter cartridge")
   Captures the user who has been cleaning a dead cartridge for two years.

8. **Why your pool service company charges so much for filter cleaning**
   Competitor-frame post. Reinforces `/pricing` positioning.

### Tier C — local and seasonal (publish third)

9. **Pollen season in Temecula Valley: filter implications**
   Low direct volume, high local-SEO and AI Overview value. Anchors the Temecula city page.

10. **How Santa Ana winds affect your pool filter**
    Authority play. Search demand spikes during actual Santa Ana events.

11. **Marine layer and salt: filter care for coastal San Diego pools**
    Anchors Coronado, Imperial Beach, Encinitas, Solana Beach city pages.

12. **Pool filter cleaning in spring: why it matters more than you think**
    Seasonal hook. Republish-able every March with a date refresh.

## Format defaults (apply to every post)

- 1,200 to 1,400 words (honest middle of the requested 1,200 to 1,800 range)
- H1 with target keyword, at least one H2 with target keyword or close variant
- One H2 that frames the local angle (Southern California, San Diego County, or a named city)
- Lead-in paragraph that names the problem directly (no "in today's world" openers)
- FAQ section with 4 questions, emitted as both visible content and FAQPage schema
- Author byline "Filter Fresh team"
- Featured-image alt text described in the frontmatter (`featured_alt`), ready for whichever image source you use
- Internal links woven into prose, not parked in a footer
- No em dashes, no en dashes, no emojis

Track in GA4:

- Page views per post

- Position for the target keyword by week 4 of indexing

- Click-through rate on title and meta description (refresh both at week 8 if CTR is below 3 percent)
